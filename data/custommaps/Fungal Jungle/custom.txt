 --=[ Installation ]=-----------------------------------------------------
Extract to the Alex 4 folder, and drag this very text file on to the
program icon to play.

 --=[ The Speech ]=-------------------------------------------------------
It's 12 maps, including a water level, an underground level, a rolling
level, and two boss levels.  The difficulty should be about right
if you've already beaten the original maps.
