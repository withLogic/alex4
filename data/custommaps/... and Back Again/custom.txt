Description:

Hello, this pack is a sequel to my first one (called Journey to the Pole). It contains 7 levels (well, if you count the last one as a real level...). The story (or "story" in fact) starts where the Journey to the Pole finished. Thinking he destroyed the evil Mr. Pounder, who wanted to turn Antarctis into a huge allegator concentration camp (while in fact he killed his also-evil twin, Mr. Bush), Alex made his escape through the tunnels under the installation in hope to find a boat to get away.
Suddenly the tunnel ends and you can feel fresh air blowing on your face again. It's time to get out of this place!

When I started making this pack, my goal was to creating something that would be easier as my first one, because people complained it was too hard. So I hope you won't find this one too easy... :)
