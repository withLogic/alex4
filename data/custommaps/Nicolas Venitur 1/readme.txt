Add-on levels for "Alex 4"

These are my levels for "Alex the Allegator 4". Just drag and drop "Nic_venitur1.txt" on a shortcut to the game and play my levels.

They have to be played in the following order:

1) Egg Shooting           egg_shootin.map
2) Aquaphobia             aquaphobia.map
3) The Chasm              the_chasm.map
4) Wohoo                  wohoo.map
5) Jellyfish Fun          jellyfish.map
6) La Bomba Blanca        bomba_blanca.map
7) Xtreme Spike Truckz    xtreme.map

My levels are quite tricky, so try to beat them!
Look ahead for 'Venitur 2'!
