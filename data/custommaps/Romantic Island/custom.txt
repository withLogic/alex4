Romantic Island is an Alex Allegator 4 custom game designed by Richard Kalkman.
The goals I had is to make a game with long but nice to play levels and with surprised elements in it.
So the total game exist of seventeen variable levels made in wonderful themes.

Explaining all the levels Alex has to:
[oase.map] cross the boring desert with oases and real flying fakirs,
[berg.map] fall in ravines and beware of the rolling stones coming down from the mountains,
[grot.map] crawl trough the holes with underground rivers and lakes,
[stad.map] stop the overpopulation in the enermies capital city and destroy them buildings,
[blik.map] go trough the terrible canned allegator factory,
[brug.map] break the ugly enermies bridges in the beautiful allegator valleys,
[rots.map] climb over and under the rocks and beware not to crunch,
[boom.map] swing likes Tarzan from tree to tree over the jungle,
[dras.map] search his way in the swamp and take care not to sink in mud or sand,
[dool.map] find the secret space ship in the enermies hidden labyrinth,
[ster.map] meat al the live in space between the asteroids and get the stars from heaven,
[wolk.map] fly in the air trough the clouds and be carefully not to hit the other flying allegators,
[pool.map] slide over high flakes of ice and break trough the ice blocks,
[kolk.map] swim in a turbulent ocean where whirlpools will rotate Alex fast far away,
[buit.map] get the jewels on Treasure Island witch are stolen by the pirates,
[dorp.map] walk trough the cannibal's village where they hold their allegator cooking pots,
[lava.map] and jump over volcano's to the Romantic Island where Alex will meat Lola again.

The levels are good playable for the advance player but sometimes it could be a little bit tricky.
There should be enough live ups to try the levels over again, however those are difficult to get.
It is just a game for those who like adventure; and the pleasure is that you can collect plenty points.

If you want to tell me anything over this game you can send me an email (richardkalkman@casema.nl).
To play this custom map you need the main game Alex the Allegator 4 (http://allegator.sourceforge.net/).
Romantic Island is designed with the green edition and should play in that one.
There is also an colored edition, but I don't believe this will looks very well. You may try it out.

Good luck by playing Romantic Island and I hope you enjoy this game.
