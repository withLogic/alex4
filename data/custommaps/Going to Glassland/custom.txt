The crocodile is called Mario and he was having fun in water in Anja (a crocodile too). Suddenly, the ground was
trembling and they thought it was earthquake. But, it wasn't earthquake. It was some jumping machine. Some jealous guy was
in it. He came out, took her and said: "Hy, you fool. I don't believe you are so strong to take your friend back because
you are small. So stupid, clumsy and very small crocodile. Hah, hah, hah!"
Mario was very angry. He saw some inscription wroten on machine: "Buy glass - very cheap!" That's it! Glassland!! 
Anja is in Glassland!!!
You must help Mario to save Anja before something really bad happens!

This levels are for my friend Anja.

Well, maybe these levels are hard to play, but with little imagination and logic you can solve every level. Have practise
and - the most of all - HAVE FUN!
