released: 11/07/03
game: Alex4 (http://www.freelunchdesign.com/)
creation time: on and off (mostly off) for about a week.

--NOTES---------------------------------------------------
1: These levels are NOT easy. They've been designed for people that are looking for another challenge having completed the main game.
2: As at the time of release only I have playtested the levels there may well be unfair bits, bugs, etc. that I dont know about.
3: My 'secrets' are generally based on the idea that the challenge should not be finding them, but reaching them safely. 
4: I LOVE feedback, and im always glad of it, even when its pretty scathing (as long as theres a good reason).  Please do send any comments, suggestions, questions and requests to me at jonathan@thewhitings.freeserve.co.uk
