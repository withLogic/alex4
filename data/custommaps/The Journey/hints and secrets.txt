Level 1

Hint:
On the first level, (where the jelly fish is) you do not need to slip threw the spikes
to get to that little island below, simply jump onto that cliff (where the jelly fish
jumps up) and quickly jump on to below that little island.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Level 2

Secret:
When starting out, head back from where you came and there will be a heart and 1up
waiting for you.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Level 4

Hints:
You do not HAVE to get the egg positioned near the top, but it could help near the end.
Near the end, you do not need to worry about the pufferfish that does not come up above
the ground tile, you can walk on it and not get hurt.
The 1up and heart at the end, is very hard to get. If you got the egg it makes it all
the easier to get it though. (if you didn't use it)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Level 5

Secret:
When you get to the mid part of the cave, (where the pedistal is) jump left (onto the top
of the level) walk to your left, jump over the gap. And you will be rewarded with cherries,
stars, and a 1up.

Hint:
You get two eggs on this level, use them near the end. It's a tight space back there
and it might be hard to jump on them with that cannon.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Level 6

Hint:
Do not roll backwards.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Level 7

Hint:
Do not kill all the little grunts that come down to you, use them to jump on the left
part of the level for many eggs. (the eggs are not needed to pass the level, though
they might make it easier)

Secret:

There are two 1ups from where you just came (over the spikes and the crushers)
